## Notes

- **LC29HDAMD** (`C20748061`, U201) -- THE cost and THE stock risk. LC29HBS (C21385845, RTK base) and LC29HAA (C22403415, no RTK engine) share the footprint
- **B39162B8389P810** (`C5556161`, FL301) -- dual-band L1+L5. Do NOT substitute an L1-only SAW -- it deletes the second band RTK depends on
- **LQW15AN5N1B00D** (`C408161`, L302, L303) -- 5.1nH is the SAW's specified port match, not a rounding of 5nH
- **LQW15AN68NG00D** (`C108965`, L301) -- wirewound, >=68nH per the Quectel reference. A ferrite bead here would eat the antenna signal
- **RF1256-000** (`C207186`, D301) -- 0.25pF junction capacitance. The LC29H guide caps this at 0.6pF -- an ordinary ESD diode detunes the antenna
